The plan

Lots of tiny games, each one finished, put online and played by people who don't know me. A game jam every month or so. Under them, the technical side: my own little engine in C with some x86-64 assembly, and a Game Boy game written in assembly. After all that come three bigger games, one at a time: a Zelda-like, a horror game and a metroidvania.
